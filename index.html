About Me

Fresh graduate. Real-world enterprise impact. Production code shipped.

I'm a 2026 B.Tech graduate in AI & Machine Learning (CGPA 8.10) from Hindusthan College of Engineering and Technology, currently working as an Application Developer at Auraliss. I'm not just a fresh grad — I'm a developer with production-shipped code and active enterprise project experience before my degree even concluded.

At Auraliss, I build across the full mobile stack. Native Android in Kotlin using Jetpack Compose — adaptive across phones and tablets — and a parallel Flutter (Dart) codebase for iOS and web multiplatform coverage. I apply MVVM architecture and Clean Architecture principles to ensure maintainable, scalable, production-grade code.

My most significant project at Auraliss is for Parry's Brown Sugar Factory (EID Parry — Murugappa Group), one of South India's largest sugar and jaggery manufacturers. I collect data from industrial instrumentation — sensors, PLCs, IoT devices on the factory floor — run it through an AI/ML pipeline, and render the results as real-time visual dashboards inside our mobile app. This is Industry 4.0 delivered at enterprise scale for one of India's most iconic conglomerates.

I thrive at the intersection of mobile engineering and industrial AI. I write clean, well-tested, documented code. I'm a fast learner, a strong team collaborator, and a developer who takes end-to-end ownership of features — from architecture decisions through deployment.
